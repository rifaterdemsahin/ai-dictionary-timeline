🎬 Core Pillar 04 / 04 • Visual Storyboards & Pre-Visualization

Previz & Storyboard Navigation Hub

The visual pre-production studio. In professional filmmaking and generative AI pipelines, Pre-visualization (Previz) validates camera motion, keyframe composition, and audio timing before burning GPU tokens on full video rendering.

💡 Strategic Rationale: Why Previz Saves 90% of Render Costs

Full AI video generation (Kling 1.6 Pro at $0.560 per video) is computationally intensive and irreversible. Rendering blind without visual storyboards results in expensive re-renders and prompt hallucinations. The Previz pillar enforces visual gating:

01 • Cheap Static Validation
Generates high-resolution 9:16 keyframes via fal.ai Seedream 4.0 ($0.030) to approve visual style and terminal wireframes before launching video interpolation.
02 • Audio-Visual Choreography
The Tell-Show-Do-Apply storyboard aligns camera motion (whip pans, snap zooms) with 4 precise Foley SFX cues and ElevenLabs audio ducking (-18 dB).
03 • Executable Anchor Spec
Compiles the complete single-video specification for Video #01 (Artifacts) with 1-click clipboard copy buttons for immediate CLI execution.
