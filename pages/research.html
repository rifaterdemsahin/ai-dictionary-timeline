Automating 24 high-definition vertical videos without rigorous market research leads to high compute token waste and negligible student acquisition. The Research pillar solves three existential failure modes in automated content creation:

02 • Unit Economics Validation
Proves that $0.632 automated YouTube Shorts deliver 6.7x higher CAC ROI than $4.20 Google Search clicks for driving AI professionals into Skool.
03 • Quality Assurance Gating
Enforces a 7-stage automated regression suite so dead links, broken schemas, or missing CTA pinned comments never enter production.
