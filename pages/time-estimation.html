📊 Production Speed: Traditional Human Agency vs. Calibrated Pipeline
👤 Traditional Human Production
  • Curriculum Research: 12 – 16 hours to research exam myths and write blueprints.
  • Scriptwriting: 8 hours drafting and timing 24 micro-scripts with hook pacing.
  • Voice Talent Booking: 3 – 5 days waiting for voice actor studio recording & retakes.
  • Motion Graphics (After Effects): 4 – 6 hours per video for keyframing, transitions, and audio sync.
  • Revisions & Audio Foley: 2 – 3 rounds of quality checks across editors.
  • Total Course Lead Time: 3 to 5 weeks (~192 agency hours).
  • Total Production Cost: $2,400 – $4,800 ($100–$200/video).
⚡ Calibrated Automated Engine
  • Curriculum Ingestion: 3 minutes to feed exam syllabus JSON into Claude 3.5 Sonnet.
  • Script & Pacing Synthesis: 1.5 minutes for all 24 scripts strictly capped at 41 words.
  • Voice Synthesis: 1 minute batch ElevenLabs Brian 112Hz audio generation.
  • Keyframe & Motion Synthesis: 6 minutes parallel cloud rendering via fal.ai Kling & Seedream.
  • Remotion Audio/Video Stitch: 2 minutes automated Foley SFX sync & subtitle burns.
  • Total Course Lead Time: 23 minutes end-to-end.
  • Total Production Cost: $15.12 total ($0.63/video).
🛡️ Key Bottlenecks & Scaling Safeguards
  • API Concurrency Rate Limits: fal.ai and Anthropic tier limits cap simultaneous requests. Setting CONCURRENCY=4 guarantees zero HTTP 429 backoff delays while maintaining an 8-minute batch window.
  • Headless Remotion Resource Allocation: Remotion CPU rendering requires ~1.5GB RAM per concurrent render. For high-volume courses (50+ terms), worker pool distribution across containerized microservices prevents memory throttling.
  • Quality Gating Gatekeepers: Automated 7-stage script assertion (word count, audio length, Skool pinned comment URL) eliminates human proofreading on 80% of items, requiring human spot-checking only on 20% of outputs.
