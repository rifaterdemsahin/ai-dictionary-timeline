✍️ Core Pillar 02 / 04 • Narrative, Prompt & Voice Engineering

Script & Curriculum Navigation Hub

The verbal and pedagogical heartbeat of the engine. In a 16-second format, every syllable must be calibrated. The Script pillar bridges raw curriculum definitions into high-retention spoken micro-lessons delivered by calibrated neural voices.

💡 Strategic Rationale: Why Scripting Requires Dedicated Systems

16-second vertical video is an unforgiving cognitive constraint: exactly 41 spoken words at 165 WPM, leaving zero room for filler words. The Script pillar provides the architecture for narrative precision:

01 • Curriculum as Structured Data
By storing the script inside claude-associate-terms.json, each video's hook, concept, trap, and CTA are version-controlled and programmatically inspectable.
02 • Acoustic Voice Engineering
Standard TTS sounds robotic. Scripting tunes ElevenLabs Brian to 112 Hz fundamental pitch and 165 WPM pacing for maximum technical credibility.
03 • Two-Stage Calibration Logic
Requires producing and calibrating Anchor Video #01 first to empirically verify audio pacing, frame sync, and viewer retention before generating the remaining 23 scripts.
